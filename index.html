FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Are your staff background-checked, bonded and insured?

Yes. All staff are background-checked, and the company is bonded and insured.

Do I need to supply cleaning supplies?

No. Our staff bring their own equipment and eco-friendly products. If you prefer specific products, just ask.

What is the difference between a regular and a deep clean?

A regular clean covers maintenance tasks. A deep clean is more intensive and is recommended for first-time clients.

How is pricing determined?

By home size (bedrooms and bathrooms), service type and frequency. Quotes are free and no-obligation.
